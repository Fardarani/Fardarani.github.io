Academic profile and professional background

Profile

Mohammadreza Ahmadi Darani is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Sciences at Shahrekord University.

His academic activities focus on computational mathematics, numerical analysis, and the development of computational methods for scientific problems.

Research Interests

His research interests include Numerical Analysis, Differential Equations, Meshless Methods, Machine Learning, and Scientific Computing.

Academic Background

His research and teaching activities involve mathematical modeling, numerical algorithms, computational techniques, and applied mathematics.

Teaching Philosophy

His teaching approach emphasizes strong mathematical foundations combined with computational examples and real-world applications.

Education

Ph.D. in Mathematics with research focus on computational mathematics, numerical methods and scientific computing.
