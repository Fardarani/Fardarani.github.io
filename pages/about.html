Academic profile and professional background

Profile

I am currently an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Sciences at Shahrekord University. I began my academic collaboration with Shahrekord University in 2010. Over the years, I have taught mathematics and engineering mathematics courses at the undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral levels. My teaching activities have focused on developing a deeper understanding of mathematical concepts and highlighting their connections to scientific and practical applications.

Research Interests

A major part of my research focuses on numerical analysis and the development of numerical methods for solving scientific problems, particularly spectral methods, meshless methods for function approximation, and numerical approaches for solving partial differential equations. Alongside my research activities, I am interested in developing and applying innovative, technology-based educational approaches and using artificial intelligence to improve the learning process, deepen the understanding of scientific concepts, and support research activities.

Academic Background

My academic activities have developed at the intersection of applied mathematics, scientific computing, and computer science. My work has primarily focused on studying and developing mathematical and computational approaches for modeling, approximation, and solving scientific problems. A significant part of these activities involves the design and analysis of numerical algorithms and the investigation of their performance in computational problems. Alongside my teaching responsibilities, I have also been involved in developing educational resources, lecture notes, and scientific materials for students. I have sought to establish meaningful connections between theoretical concepts, computational methods, and practical needs throughout my academic activities. This approach has provided a foundation for the development of my research and teaching activities in computational mathematics and its applications. educational content and instructional packages.

Teaching Philosophy

In my teaching activities, I emphasize strengthening mathematical foundations and developing a deep and conceptual understanding of the subject matter. I seek to connect theoretical concepts with computational examples, practical applications, and real-world problems so that students can better appreciate the relevance of mathematics to its applications. The use of modern educational approaches and emerging technologies, including electronic content and interactive tools, is also an important part of my teaching activities. I strive to create a dynamic and collaborative learning environment that encourages students to learn, ask questions, and explore new ideas. I consider motivating students and strengthening their confidence in approaching mathematical and scientific problems to be an essential part of the learning process. My goal is to help students transform theoretical knowledge into problem-solving skills and apply what they have learned effectively in their academic and professional activities.
