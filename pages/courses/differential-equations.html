Differential Equations

Analytical and numerical methods for ordinary and partial differential equations

Course Description

This course introduces fundamental concepts and methods for solving ordinary differential equations and provides an introduction to partial differential equations.

Topics

First Order ODEs, Higher Order ODEs, Systems of Differential Equations, Laplace Transform, Numerical Methods, Partial Differential Equations

Lecture Materials

Lecture notes, slides and educational materials for differential equations.

Computational Resources

MATLAB examples, numerical simulations and supplementary exercises.
