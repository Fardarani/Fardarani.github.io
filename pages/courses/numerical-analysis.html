Course Overview

Numerical Analysis is an undergraduate course focused on computational methods for solving mathematical problems that arise in science and engineering.

The course introduces approximation techniques and numerical algorithms for problems where analytical solutions are difficult or impossible to obtain.

Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to develop numerical algorithms, analyze computational errors, implement numerical methods using MATLAB, and apply approximation techniques to engineering and scientific problems.

Syllabus

Course Outline:

  1. Introduction to Numerical Analysis and Computational Mathematics
  2. Error Analysis, Accuracy and Stability
  3. Interpolation Methods
  4. Curve Fitting and Least Squares Approximation
  5. Numerical Differentiation
  6. Numerical Integration
  7. Nonlinear Equations and Root Finding Methods
  8. Numerical Methods for Ordinary Differential Equations
